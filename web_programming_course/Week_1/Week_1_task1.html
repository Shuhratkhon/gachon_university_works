How to Make Kimchi Fried Rice

What You Need

  • 1 cup aged kimchi (chopped)
  • 2 cups cooked cold rice
  • 2 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 fried egg (for topping)

Steps

  1. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a tablespoon of cooking oil.
  2. Add chopped kimchi and stir-fry until soft for about 3 minutes.
  3. Add cooked cold rice and kimchi juice; stir vigorously to mix evenly.
  4. Warning: Turn off heat before drizzle of sesame oil to avoid burning it.
  5. Top with a fried egg and serve hot immediately.
